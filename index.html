01our story

we are little fox and we make ice cream that some people really, really love.

Little Fox was born in 2020, when the whole world was stuck at home. We wanted to lift some spirits and help people find their way back to each other — so we started making ice cream. A Penn State ice cream short course and a few conferences later, we're still learning, and still obsessed.

Sometimes people say our flavors are a little off-beat and unique. That's true! We look for inspiration from outside the ice cream world to dream up new ideas. It really keeps things interesting.

01

everything from scratch

We separate eggs by hand and make every base, swirl and crumble ourselves. It's slower and a lot more work. It's so worth it.

02

local farms, real food

Organic strawberries, carrots, beets and tomatoes from farms nearby, and milk from a local, certified-humane dairy.

03

off-beat on purpose

Root beer floats, sourdough, goat cheese, garam masala. Every flavor starts with a story we can't stop thinking about.
